About Manindra de Mel

    
MDMI build software that leaves the lab. That means AI products people carry in their pockets, vision models trained on messy field data, and back-end systems that keep payments moving. I work small, ship end to end, and stay on call after launch.


    

      
Products
Apps taken from sketch to store, with live data, streaming generation, payments and subscriptions.

      
Models
Fine-tuning and evaluation for vision models that have to work on real field data.

      
Systems
Payments data pipelines, ordering platforms and analytics, run in production.

    


    

      
      

        
Most projects reach me in one of three shapes. A product that has to go from sketch to App Store. A model that has to hold up on real-world data. A system that has to stay up while the business grows around it. I scope it, build it and run it, usually as the only engineer.
